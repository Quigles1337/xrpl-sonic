Every body is earned by data: a planet is an account that appeared in a validated transaction the page heard; a star issues a currency to at least three holders in those records; a black hole candidate has its master key disabled and an unusable regular key in its account root; a pulsar has a measured cadence. Every body's label prints the threshold that made it. A planet is named by its on-ledger Domain, else by the label the listening page's cast gives it (a cast member wears a ring in its cast colour), else by its short address. The coverage line says how much was heard. Nothing is stored; reloading starts an empty sky.

With prefers-reduced-motion the sky stands still: no slow turn, comets as still lines that fade.
