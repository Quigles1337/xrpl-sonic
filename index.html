    xrpl-sonic

    Every transaction on the XRP Ledger becomes a sound. Every ledger close, a pulse.

    Your browser connects directly to a public XRPL node over WebSocket. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Sound starts after you press Start; turn your volume down first.

    Source on GitHub · Space: start/stop · M: mute
