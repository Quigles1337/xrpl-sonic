# Universe: requests to shared modules

The universe session creates `universe.html`, `src/universe/`, `docs/universe.md` and `test/universe*` and edits nothing else. Everything the spike needed from a shared module is listed here as a request, with the exact function and why; for the spike each need was met by a copy under `src/universe/` or by importing the module read-only. The owner of each module decides. Nothing here is urgent for the spike; all of it matters for phase 1 of [universe.md](universe.md).

## Phase 2's findings on the open requests

- **R4** is served as filed: `frameFromLive`'s `cast` option takes a list of any length (`accountsFor` returns the cast whole). The sky still copies the frame builder, for the involved reading (R17).
- **R14**: the loader has not landed on this branch; the sky pages `account_tx` newest first itself (`src/universe/probe.js` `fetchRecentHistory`, the shared fetcher's pacing), because `src/account-tx.js` pages oldest first and a busy hub's recent ledgers run out of pages before they reach the window. The loader's entrance should take `forward: false`.
- **R16**: the readout is the sky's own panel (`#probe` in `universe.html`), styled like the listening page's drawer; the shared drawer mounts on the listening page's markup and stylesheet.

## Not requested, by design

- No change to `src/explorer/constellation.js`: the sky does not reuse the constellation's drawing; it reuses its camera (R1) and its vocabulary (orbits, beads, stars) in [universe.md](universe.md).
- No change to the case format: a sky position is not part of a case; a tour (phase 4) will be a case with annotations, and whether a case gains a `camera` field is an owner's decision listed in [universe.md](universe.md).
- No change to `scripts/describe.mjs`: the sky's text equivalent (`describeBody`) lives in `src/universe/bodies.js`; a Node entrance for it is phase 2 work.
