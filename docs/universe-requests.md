# Universe: requests to shared modules

The universe session creates `universe.html`, `src/universe/`, `docs/universe.md` and `test/universe*` and edits nothing else. Everything the spike needed from a shared module is listed here as a request, with the exact function and why; for the spike each need was met by a copy under `src/universe/` or by importing the module read-only. The owner of each module decides. Nothing here is urgent for the spike; all of it matters for phase 1 of [universe.md](universe.md).

| # | Module | Request | Why | What the spike does meanwhile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | `src/projection.js` | Export a `viewMatrix(camera, viewport)` (or the yaw/pitch/distance/target/focal as one object) that a WebGL vertex shader can consume, built from the same `toCamera` and `focal` the 2D paths use. | The sky's WebGL path re-derives the camera in GLSL so that both renderers project a point to the same pixel; a shared source of the numbers keeps the plate, the constellation and the sky in one camera when a change comes. | `src/universe/projection.js` is a verbatim copy of the module; `src/universe/sky-gl.js` evaluates the same rotation in its vertex shader with uniforms set from the camera object. |
| R2 | `src/moment.js` (`LiveWindow`) | Let `LiveWindow` take a `max` above 50 for a reader that does not save a case, or expose the per-ledger `accounts` and `touches` without copying through the `ledgers` getter. | The sky's coherence (pulsars, pairs in step) runs `frameFromLive` over the window every ten ledgers; the getter copies every account map and every touch record each time. The 50-ledger cap is the case cap, which the sky does not need. | The worker calls `frameFromLive(live)` every ten closed ledgers and accepts the copy; the 50-ledger window stands. |
| R3 | `src/coherence.js` | Export the cadence per account independent of pairs: `cadences(frame) → Map<address, { period, r } \| null>`, reusing `cadenceOf` and `normalise`. | A pulsar is an account's own cadence, not a property of a pair; today the only path to a cadence is through `pairCoherence`, so an account that is not in a judged pair has none, and the worker reads cadences off `pair.cadence.a/b`. | The worker reads the cadences off the judged pairs of the top-8 submitters; a pulsar outside those pairs is not drawn, and the spec says so. |
| R4 | `src/coherence.js` | Let `frameFromLive` (or `accountsFor`) take an explicit account list beyond the cast: `{ accounts: [address, …] }`, so a caller can ask for the accounts it is looking at (the focused planet and its counterparties). | Exploration (phase 2 of the universe) opens a planet and wants its pairs with its counterparties, not with the top-K submitters of the whole window. | Not needed by the spike; recorded for phase 2. |
| R5 | `src/record.js` | Export the AccountRoot reader the sky needs beside `touchOf`: `rootsOf(meta) → [{ address, action, balance, flags, regularKey, domain, ammId, ownerCount }]`, and a `linesOf(meta)` with `issuerOfLine(line)`. | Three body rules (planet size from the balance, black hole from flags and key, binary star from AMMID; star from the issuer side of a RippleState) read the metadata that `touchOf` already walks; one walk should serve both, and the record module is where a transaction's metadata is read into facts. | `src/universe/records.js` has `rootsOf`, `linesOf`, `issuerOfLine` and `offersOf` of its own, proven on the facts fixtures. |
| R6 | `src/explorer/facts.js` | A rule id and tier for the issuer side of a trust line (`line.issuer.balance`, calculated; `line.issuer.limit`, rule) in the rule catalogue, so the sky's star rule cites a catalogued rule. | The rule is the sky's: the balance sign (or at zero, which side set a limit) names the issuer. Putting it in the catalogue makes "why is this a star?" a lookup like every explorer sentence. | The ids are used in `src/universe/records.js` and documented in [universe.md](universe.md); they are not yet in the catalogue. |
| R7 | `src/explorer/account.js` | Let `loadAccount` be called with `{ parts: ['info', 'lines'] }` to fetch a subset, and return `account_objects` filtered to `SignerList`. | Exploration opens a planet: it needs the trust lines (rings) and the root (size, flags) first, the rest later; the black hole candidate becomes a rule only when `account_objects` shows no SignerList. | Not needed by the spike (live stream only). |
| R8 | `src/explorer/sigil.js` | Export `sigilHue(address)` beside `sigilColour`, or make `sigilPattern` cheap enough to call 20,000 times a ledger. | A planet's hue is its sigil hue; `sigilPattern` builds the 25 cells and a BigInt for every call. | `src/universe/bodies.js` computes `hashString(address) % 360`, which is the same number `sigilPattern` uses; a test pins the equality. |
| R9 | `src/audio.js` | A `play(event)` that accepts the minimal event shape the worker posts (`category, sound, variant, pitch, freq, notes, velocity, duration, sub, timbre`), documented as the contract, or a `minimalEvent(event)` helper in `src/mapping.js`. | The worker computes `txToEvent` and posts only the fields the engine reads; if the engine starts reading a new field the sky's sound breaks silently. | The spike posts those fields and the engine plays them. |
| R10 | `src/mapping.js` | Export `LAYER_OF_CATEGORY` (payment, trust, DEX, NFT) or a `layerOf(category)` beside `categorize`, if the layers of the sky are wanted on the listening page too. | The layer toggles of the sky group the eight categories into four; the grouping should live with the categories. | `src/universe/sky.js` and `sky-gl.js` each carry the table. |
| R11 | `src/ws.js` | Expose the bytes received (`onRaw` is enough, and exists) and the subscribe result's `hostid` on the status callback. | The coverage line names the host; the subscribe response carries the node's hostid, which the status object does not. | The worker names the endpoint host from the status callback. |
| R12 | `index.html` / `docs/ux.md` | A place for the sky in the mode map: a fourth door, or Universe as a page beside Explorer in every mode's top bar. | The sky is a page of its own (`universe.html`) and is reached from nowhere today. | The spike's page links back to the listening page and the explorer; nothing links to it. |

## Not requested, by design

- No change to `src/explorer/constellation.js`: the sky does not reuse the constellation's drawing; it reuses its camera (R1) and its vocabulary (orbits, beads, stars) in [universe.md](universe.md).
- No change to the case format: a sky position is not part of a case; a tour (phase 4) will be a case with annotations, and whether a case gains a `camera` field is an owner's decision listed in [universe.md](universe.md).
- No change to `scripts/describe.mjs`: the sky's text equivalent (`describeBody`) lives in `src/universe/bodies.js`; a Node entrance for it is phase 2 work.
